I think I speak for both of us when I say that we usually don't pack for a vacation several days in advance, but rather 1-2 days before. Often even on the same day.
For the upcoming Yukon adventure, however, there are still a few things to consider in advance.

For example, I bought a new camera a few months ago so that we can take even better photos on the trip. This also includes an additional battery, a camera cage (to attach, for example, an iPhone), and a capture clip. This makes it possible to hang the camera outside the backpack during a hike and always have it ready at hand.

Regarding clothing, packing will be a challenge. We try to keep it minimalist and mainly pack sportswear and hiking clothes. However, the temperatures between Calgary, Whitehorse, and Inuvik are very different. In Calgary, we will encounter summer-like temperatures of around 30 degrees Celsius. Whitehorse is already significantly cooler at about 20 degrees Celsius. The further north we travel, the colder it gets. In Tuktoyaktuk, we can expect about between 3 and 14 degrees Celsius.   
Starting from Whitehorse, I think hiking shoes will be used predominantly. For the flights and the days in Calgary, a pair of sneakers will suffice.   
I think that several pairs of shorts will probably not be necessary. On the other hand, it is better to pack a rain jacket and some normal pants.

Besides the default equipment such as a headlamp, pocket knife, mosquito spray, and power bank, a small coffee maker should not be missing either.   
The rest of the camping equipment is part of the booked vehicle and we will receive it in Whitehorse when we pick it up. Oh yes, I still have to request the shuttle from Whitehorse Airport to the rental station...

As always, the rule applies this time as well: pack less. Most of the time, there are always washing machines somewhere (e.g., in our two Calgary accommodations).

Fun fact: Our two accommodations in Calgary are again in the same area (Radisson Heights) as in 2024 when we lived there for 3 months.
