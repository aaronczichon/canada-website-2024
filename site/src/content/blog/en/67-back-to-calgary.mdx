We divided our trip into 4 main sections.

1. Arrival in Calgary + onward flight to Whitehorse
2. Camping trip all the way to the Arctic Ocean
3. Returning the vehicle + days in Whitehorse
4. Things to do in Calgary and flight home

When we booked the flights and camper on New Year's Eve 2025/26, this seemed like a good plan. By spending a short amount of time in Calgary before and after the trip, we could get somewhat used to the time difference and maybe show Fredi and Christian a little bit of Calgary as well. The thought of having a proper bed and a shower before and after camping also seemed like a good idea to us.    
Whether dividing the trip into these sections was really as sensible and good as we had imagined, we'll of course only tell you at the end. But let's start at the beginning.

<DirectusGallery folderName="67 Back to Calgary" />

So: Welcome (back) to Calgary!   
When we arrived back in Canada, we had planned 2 nights, meaning one full day, in Calgary. We weren't sure whether taking a connecting flight straight away would have made much sense without having to spend ages waiting at the airport. It probably would have been tight to catch a domestic flight directly after arriving in Calgary. When arriving internationally in Calgary and continuing on a domestic flight, you first have to go through the required checks. So we probably wouldn't have made a direct connection.      
There isn't much to say about the flight from Munich to Calgary. And that's explicitly meant as a good thing. The flight wasn't delayed and we arrived in Calgary on time.   

Since the weather in Calgary is usually wonderful during the summer, we were still able to make good use of our summer clothes during those days.   
Of course, the -8 h time difference from Central European Time is a little difficult to cope with, and later on our arrival day we really started struggling with tiredness.   
Still, we didn't want to miss out on a “classic” ~~American~~ Canadian meal. So, of course, we had burgers + poutine.   
We also used our one full day before the next flight to stop by MEC (Mountain Equipment Canada), an outdoor store, and pick up a few last-minute things.   
We even got to see one or two meerkats. At that point, we didn't yet know that we would see (almost) no other animals...
